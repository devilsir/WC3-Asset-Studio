# WC3 Asset Studio v1.1.2

## Geoset editing

Model Lab now includes a dedicated **Geosets** workspace. Geosets can be selected from the viewport or the list, hidden individually, focused, moved by axis, dragged freely in the view plane, scaled by axis or uniformly, and rotated around X/Y/Z.

Geoset transforms edit bind-pose geometry and update vertex normals and model/geoset extents. Saving the model writes those geometry changes back to MDX or MDL. Geoset editing also has its own undo/redo history with up to 25 states.

Texture quality/resolution export, texture rename/path synchronization, Texture Paint loading fixes and the expanded WC3 Buttons options from the previous v1.1.1 work remain included.
